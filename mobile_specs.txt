Engine 
The GPS Geofencing Attendance Engine replaces unreliable paper registers and fixed biometric hardware 
with an event-driven, mobile-native geospatial verification pipeline.

6.1 How GPS Geofencing Works in Practice 
• 
1. Site Geofence Creation: Operations selects the client facility on the web map or inputs surveyed GPS 
latitude and longitude coordinates. 
• 
2. Perimeter Configuration: Operations sets a circular geofence boundary around the physical facility (e.g., 
50 meters, 100 meters, or custom perimeter based on site area). 
• 
3. Location Acquisition: The assigned employee arrives on-site and opens the Employee Mobile App to 
select 'Punch In'. 
• 
4. Backend Geospatial Math: The app captures the high-accuracy device GPS coordinates along with 
reported accuracy radius and dispatches a signed payload over HTTPS. 
• 
5. Boundary Evaluation: The backend computes the exact spherical distance between the device 
coordinates and the configured site center using the Haversine formula.

6. Verification Outcome: If the calculated distance is less than or equal to the site radius, the punch is

attendance ledger, and the Client Mobile App dashboard. 
6.2 Technical Safeguards, Privacy & Exception Handling 
BATTERY & PRIVACY ARCHITECTURE: To preserve mobile battery life, respect employee privacy, and 
comply with mobile OS background execution restrictions, the platform utilizes Event-Based Geolocation 
Checks. GPS hardware is queried strictly at the exact moments the employee taps 'Punch In' or 'Punch Out'. 
Continuous background tracking is deliberately avoided. 
 
• 
Anti-Spoofing & Mock Location: The mobile app checks for mock location flags and developer-mode GPS 
spoofing tools on the operating system, rejecting punches generated by spoofed coordinates. 
• 
Accuracy Threshold Filtering: Every GPS payload includes an accuracy metric (in meters). If GPS signal 
degradation occurs (e.g. inside a basement or reinforced concrete structure) exceeding the configured 
tolerance threshold, the app alerts the user to step into an open area. 
• 
Supervisor Exception Workflow: In rare cases of genuine device hardware failure or total GPS 
unavailability, field supervisors can submit a manual attendance adjustment. Every manual adjustment 
mandates an explicit reason code, supervisor ID, and permanent audit entry. 
• 
Duplicate Punch Prevention: The engine enforces state validation to prevent accidental duplicate punch-
ins or unclosed shifts, maintaining attendance record integrity.
